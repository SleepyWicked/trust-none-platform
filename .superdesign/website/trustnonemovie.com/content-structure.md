# Trust None — Wicked Entertainment Network

## Business Context
- **Type/Industry:** Independent film distribution / Chicago-based entertainment
- **What they do:** Produce and distribute "Trust None," a gangster film set in Chicago exploring street culture and redemption
- **Target audience:** Film enthusiasts, Chicago audiences, streaming viewers, hip-hop/urban culture demographics
- **Page goal:** Drive awareness and sales across VOD, DVD, and merchandise; funnel to "Watch Now" and shop

# Page Layout & Structure

### Hero
Full-viewport background video (Chicago theater exterior, street scene); play-button overlay centered. White sans-serif stacked h1 "TRUST NONE" top-left, white subtitle "On Demand & on DVD" below. Right-aligned callout text in white: two quoted paragraphs (Chicago redemption premise + "Music, Mischief and Mayhem" hook), attribution to Che "Rhymefest" Smith in smaller caps. White horizontal accent line sits below attribution. Social icon row (Tumblr, Instagram, Vimeo, YouTube, Twitter, Facebook, search) clustered bottom-right in white. Near-black background page + white text/accents throughout.

### Header / Navigation
Relative position (not sticky). Logo (Wicked Entertainment Network wordmark + "Trust None" lockup) top-left in white. 10-link vertical navigation on left margin: Home, Videos, Synopsis, Cast & Crew, News, WATCH NOW!, Shop + 3 unlabeled social links. Hamburger menu icon top-right (white). No primary CTA in header — action deferred to "Watch Now" link in nav.

### Primary CTA / Input
"WATCH NOW!" link in navigation menu; styled as nav item (white text, accent inheritance from nav context). Routes to `/watch-now/watch-now/`. Secondary shop link below it.

### Tagline & Attribution Block
- **Purpose:** Reinforce film's core message and creator credibility
- **Layout:** Right-aligned text column over video: two 2–3 line paragraphs, attribution line in small caps, horizontal accent rule below

### Social Icon Row
- **Purpose:** Drive traffic to off-site social channels and VOD platform
- **Layout:** Horizontal cluster, bottom-right: 7 icon links (Tumblr, Instagram, Vimeo, YouTube, Twitter, Facebook, search), white on transparency

### Production Credits Block
- **Purpose:** Full cast, crew, and production company attribution (legal/marketing)
- **Layout:** Left-aligned dense text block, near-black background, white type; single column spanning page width; includes studio names, director, cast, producer credits, and R rating stamp

### Footer Navigation & Legal
- **Purpose:** Policy access and company attribution
- **Layout:** 3 items (Privacy Policy, Contact Us) + copyright line + Wicked Entertainment parent link; left-aligned white text on near-black

### Footer Logo
Lockup repeated (Wicked Entertainment Network + Trust None branding), white, left-aligned

**Notable patterns:** Video hero with overlay text (no image carousel); white text + near-black ground throughout; horizontal accent rules used sparingly; social icons as primary distribution mechanism; no visible grid sections — single-column layout with asymmetric text placement; :focus-visible active; prefers-reduced-motion respected
