# Cast & Crew – Trust None Movie

## Business Context
- **Type/Industry:** Film / Entertainment — Chicago gangster film production
- **What they do:** Showcase cast and crew biographies for "Trust None," a Chicago hip-hop gangster film
- **Target audience:** Film fans, hip-hop community, industry professionals
- **Page goal:** Display cast/crew profiles with social links and merchandise upsell

# Page Layout & Structure

### Hero
Near-black background with white "CAST & CREW" h1 positioned top-left, left-aligned. Film title "TRUST NONE" + tagline "On Demand & On DVD" visible in header area above. Horizontal scrolling carousel of 4 cast member cards (1.37:1 aspect ratio thumbnails) with names overlaid in white text bottom-left of each image. Left/right navigation arrows visible. No decorative elements beyond the carousel itself.

### Header / Navigation
Logo "TRUST NONE" (white type on near-black, linked to homepage) + hamburger menu top-right. Vertical navigation menu (10 links, collapsed behind hamburger). No sticky behavior. White text on near-black background.

### Cast/Crew Profile Cards ×9
- **Purpose:** Individual bios for 9 cast/crew members (Eric "Lil E" Brown, E.C Illa aka White Folks, Konee Rok, Buk of Psychodrama, Psyde of Psychodrama, The Legendary Traxster, Big Rap, Crucial Conflict, Cap 1)
- **Layout:** Each profile = h2 name/role + 2–4 body paragraphs of biography. Social links (Twitter, Instagram, Facebook) rendered as plain-text label + linked handle (white text, no icon). When merchandise exists (E.C Illa, Traxster): 2–4 product cards follow (each = linked album/poster image, 1.56:1 or 1.00:1 aspect ratio, + "Add to Cart" link). Collapsible section (expandable "+" toggle) between profiles.

### Profile Product Cards ×8
- **Purpose:** Sell albums and posters tied to cast members
- **Layout:** Merchandise images displayed 2–4 per profile (linked to shop). Image aspect ratios: 1.56:1 (albums), 1.00:1 (poster), 0.82:1 (variant). "Add to Cart" link in white below each image.

### Social Footer Strip
Icons + links (Tumblr, Instagram, Vimeo, YouTube, Facebook, Twitter, Search) aligned horizontally, white on near-black, positioned bottom-right of page.

### Footer
Near-black background. Logo divider (white bars). Social links repeat.

**Notable patterns:** Collapsible toggles ("+" separators) between each profile create vertical rhythm. Social links rendered as text + linked handle, not icons. Product upsells integrated within profile sections, not separate. Near-black background throughout (99.4% coverage) with white text (44 instances) as sole foreground. Rounded corners on interactive elements; pills on some controls. Reduced-motion honored via prefers-reduced-motion. Focus-visible states present.
