# Trust None — Shop

## Business Context
- **Type/Industry:** Independent film distribution & merchandise
- **What they do:** Sell physical & digital merchandise for "Trust None," a Chicago gangster film
- **Target audience:** Film fans, collectors, streaming viewers
- **Page goal:** Display & sell merchandise inventory

# Page Layout & Structure

### Hero
Full-viewport background image (Uptown theater storefront, desaturated); white logo lockup (TRUST NONE + tagline "On Demand & on DVD") pinned top-left; centered h1 "SHOP" in white, all-caps, letter-spaced, positioned mid-frame over the theater sign. Accent white underline beneath heading. No floating cards or CTAs in hero—the image dominates. Interaction: none; pure atmospheric entry.

### Header / Navigation
Fixed vertical nav positioned top-left. Logo lockup (linked home) sits above 10 link items: Home, Videos, Synopsis, Cast & Crew, News, Watch Now (primary action, white text on near-black), Shop (current, active). No hamburger; links always visible. No secondary CTA. White text on near-black background.

### Primary CTA / Input
"WATCH NOW" link in navigation row—white text, no button chrome, positioned as 6th nav item. Signals immediate access to film; secondary to Shop page mission.

### Product Grid
- **Purpose:** Display merchandise for purchase (DVDs, apparel, digital access)
- **Layout:** Uniform grid of product cards; 8 items visible. Each card: thumbnail image (1.50:1 ratio, rounded corners), title below, price, add-to-cart button (white text, rounded pill, near-black background).

### Social & Footer
- **Purpose:** Link to external channels (Tumblr, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, Vimeo, Facebook); copyright & legal
- **Layout:** Horizontal icon row (6 icons, white on near-black) positioned bottom-right of page. Footer: near-black band, white text; centered copyright line + Privacy Policy & Contact links.

**Notable patterns:** Hero image dominates first impression; h1 "SHOP" centered over architectural landmark creates editorial, location-rooted identity. Grid uses uniform 1.50:1 thumbnails throughout. White-on-black color scheme (inverted accent) drives contrast. No background alternation. :focus-visible and prefers-reduced-motion supported.
