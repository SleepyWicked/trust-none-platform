# Trust None – Watch Now

## Business Context
- **Type/Industry:** Independent film distribution / streaming platform
- **What they do:** On-demand and purchase access to "Trust None," a Chicago gangster film
- **Target audience:** Film enthusiasts, crime drama viewers
- **Page goal:** Drive conversion to watch full movie via external player link

# Page Layout & Structure

### Hero
Full-viewport background image (Chicago storefront, 16:9 aspect); centered overlay stack: "WATCH NOW!" label in small caps (white) centered top, horizontal rule below, embedded iframe (video player displaying error state "Sorry / This video does not exist" in near-black box, ~560px wide, 16:9), centered below player. Visual weight is on the player error message; asymmetry comes from logo + nav positioned top-left outside the hero zone. Main color: white text on near-black overlay; accent: white horizontal rules (top and below player). Primary interaction: iframe player (currently unplayable state); secondary: "Watch Full Movie Now!" link below.

### Header / Navigation
Sticky horizontal nav, top-left logo ("TRUST NONE" masthead + tagline "On Demand & On DVD" in white, 1.37:1 aspect), relative positioning (not fixed). 10 vertical-stack nav links below logo on left; no CTA button in nav. Hamburger icon top-right. White text on transparent near-black bg. No accent emphasis on links.

### Primary CTA / Input
"Watch Full Movie Now!" link positioned centered below player error message, white text, underlined, hyperlinks to http://Watch.TrustNoneMovie.Com. No button styling—plain text link.

### Post-Player Context
- **Purpose:** Communicate viewing status and metadata
- **Layout:** Centered column below player: small-caps label "After the trailer you will be able to watch On Demand or Purchase the Movie for Download" (white, ~72px width constraint), followed by "Watch Full Movie Now!" link separator, then 2-line metadata row: "DATE MAY 21, 2015" + "CATEGORY WATCH NOW" in small caps (gray text, smaller scale).

### Footer Social + Legal
- **Purpose:** Direct distribution across platforms; copyright + navigation
- **Layout:** Full-width near-black bg. Left side: Wicked Entertainment logo link (1.37:1). Center: full credits block (white text, body-small, 2–3 lines) listing production crew & R rating. Right side: 6 social icon links (Tumblr, Instagram, YouTube, Vimeo, Twitter, Facebook) as pill shapes, white outline. Below: 2-link footer nav (Privacy Policy, Contact Us) + copyright year + "Powered by Wicked Entertainment" link.

**Notable patterns:** Centered content axis throughout; white text exclusively on near-black bg; horizontal rule decorative element repeated (top of "WATCH NOW!" label, below it); social links clustered as icon-only pills; metadata uses small caps + gray tone for hierarchy; no grid sections—linear vertical stack.
